Jan — May 2026 · Undergraduate dissertation (individual)

Instruction Effects on the Attentional Blink for Negative Words

Investigated whether emotion-focused instructions affected the identification of negative words during the attentional blink.

  • Designed a 2 × 2 rapid serial visual presentation experiment, manipulating instruction type and target lag.
  • Implemented the experiment in PsychoPy and Pavlovia; measured second-target accuracy conditional on correct first-target identification (T2 | T1).
  • Evaluated the implications for emotional processing and attentional control.

PsychoPy · Pavlovia · Experimental design

Jan — May 2025 · Coursework research

Impact of Distinction Bias on Decision and Language Learning

Examined how comparisons between options can influence artificial-vocabulary learning.

  • Collected more than 150 questionnaire responses using picture-matching tasks, spelling and multiple-choice questions.
  • Used Python for data cleaning and SPSS for statistical analysis.
  • Considered how decision-making bias mechanisms apply to learning contexts.

Python · SPSS · Survey research
