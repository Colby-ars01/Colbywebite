A familiar lens.
With its own limits.

The Big Mac index compares the price of a similar product across countries. A positive raw valuation means a Big Mac costs more than in the base country after currency conversion; a negative value means less.

Adjusting for income offers another perspective. In July 2026, the matched median absolute gap against the US was 26.7% raw and 24.5% adjusted. The exploration below keeps both views available, alongside country histories and changes between readings.

A simple purchasing-power comparison. The index does not establish the causes of price differences.
