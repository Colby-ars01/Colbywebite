A little more about me.

I’m Keyu Zhu — you can call me Colby. I grew up in Shanghai and now live in London. I’m interested in people, research, and the ways data can help us understand human behaviour.

I studied Psychology at the University of Warwick before joining UCL’s School of Management. My research experience spans attention and emotion, decision-making and language learning, and donor mental health. Internships in user research and public affairs have also given me experience working with feedback, content and audience data.

I’m currently studying People Analytics and Human-Centric Management at UCL’s School of Management in London.
